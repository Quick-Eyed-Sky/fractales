Fractales — How to launch the app
=================================

Fractales is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer account.
So the first time you open it, macOS shows a security warning.
This is normal: the app isn't dangerous, you just need to allow it, once.

You need a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.

0. Move "Fractales.app" to your Applications folder (optional, but tidier).

METHOD 1 (works on every recent macOS)
--------------------------------------
1. Double-click "Fractales.app". macOS says it can't be opened: click "Done" (or "OK").
2. Open System Settings (Apple menu > System Settings).
3. Go to "Privacy & Security".
4. Scroll down to the Security section: you'll see a message like
   "Fractales was blocked to protect your Mac".
5. Click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
6. Fractales opens. From now on, a normal double-click is enough.

METHOD 2 (macOS 14 Sonoma only)
-------------------------------
1. Right-click (or Ctrl-click) "Fractales.app".
2. Choose "Open" from the menu.
3. In the warning window, click "Open" to confirm.

The app is in English, or in French on a Mac set to French.
